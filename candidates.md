# Candidates — The Robot Apology

Competition: `u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59`
Brief: exactly two lines. Line one: a robot apologizes for one specific, human-like small mistake, precise in its details. Line two: what it learned, derived from that mistake — not a generic promise to do better. Character counts include the newline between the two lines.

## Candidate 1 — SELECTED (260 characters)

I am sorry: I fed the cat at 6:00 exactly as scheduled, and again at 6:04, because she asked with her face and I had no rule for faces.
I have learned that a request repeated is not a new request, so from now on I will check my own log before I check her bowl.

Why it wins: the mistake is the most specific of the set (fed at 6:00, again at 6:04, defeated by a face it has no rule for), and the lesson is an operating rule that grows directly out of the failure — a repeated request is not a new request; check the log before the bowl. It sounds like a machine honestly auditing itself, which is exactly the brief's voice.
